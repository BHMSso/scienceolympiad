Bernardo Heights Science Olympiad

A high school–run program coaching Bernardo Heights Middle School students through Division B Science Olympiad — life science, earth science, physics, engineering, and inquiry events, built and studied together every season.

What we do

Updated as the season develops
01 · Compete

BHMS students field a full roster across Division B, from Anatomy & Physiology to Codebusters, competing at regional and state tournaments throughout the year.

02 · Coach

Run entirely by high schoolers — we plan practices, run build sessions for events like Boomilever and Scrambler, and coach our middle schoolers through every event category.

03 · Study

Officers share notes, practice tests, and study resources for every event with our BHMS students — all posted to the Event Resources page as the season goes on.
