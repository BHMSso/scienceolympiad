Bernardo Heights Science Olympiad

A student-run team where BHMS students compete across life science, earth science, physics, engineering, and inquiry events — building, testing, and studying together every season.

What we do

Updated as the season develops
01 · Compete

We field a full roster across all five Science Olympiad divisions, from Anatomy & Physiology to Codebusters, competing at regional and state tournaments throughout the year.

02 · Build

Engineering events like Boomilever, Scrambler, and Elastic Launched Gliders mean regular build sessions, testing, and iteration outside of normal class time.

03 · Study

Officers and teammates share notes, practice tests, and study resources for every event — all posted to the Event Resources page as the season goes on.
